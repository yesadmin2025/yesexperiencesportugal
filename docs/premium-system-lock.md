# Premium System Lock

This contract governs active public YES Experiences Portugal surfaces. It extends the established brand; it does not replace it.

## Typography

- **Fraunces** is the only editorial family: Display XL, Display, H1, H2 and H3.
- Principal headings are upright. Fraunces italic is reserved for short, meaningful emphasis inside a heading.
- **Inter** is the only body and interface family: Body Large, Body, Small, Eyebrow and Button.
- Use semantic classes: `t-display-xl`, `t-display`, `t-h1`, `t-h2`, `t-h3`, `t-body-lg`, `t-body`, `t-small`, `t-eyebrow`, `t-button`.
- Public surfaces must not depend on Montserrat, Kaushan Script, Georgia, Cormorant or Newsreader. Email-safe typography and non-public legacy/admin tools are exempt.
- Letter spacing is zero for editorial headings and body copy. Tracking is reserved for uppercase Eyebrow and Button text.

## CTA vocabulary

- Studio: **Design your day**
- Signature discovery: **Explore Signature Experiences**; **Explore Signature days** only where space is constrained
- Signature booking: **Reserve this day**
- Existing-day adjustment: **Tailor this day** or **Refine this day**
- Travel Designer: **Design my journey**
- Moments: **Plan a special moment**
- Corporate: **Plan a private group experience**
- Final Studio reveal: **Love this day · Reserve it**

Labels describe the destination or action consistently. Routes, handlers and commercial rules remain their own source of truth.

## Motion tiers

- **Micro — 140–200ms:** hover, press, focus and immediate feedback.
- **Interface/editorial — 300–450ms:** reveals, cards, drawers and ordinary transitions.
- **Cinematic — 700–1000ms:** homepage Hero and rare major chapter moments only.
- Ordinary sections use one dominant motion idea. Do not stack masks, split lines, parallax and magnetic movement.
- Checkout is immediate and quiet. Reduced-motion removes ornamental animation and never hides content.

## Flow grammar

The public journey is: inspire → orient → choose → personalize → reassure → reserve.

Studio keeps its existing phases and business logic but communicates three mental chapters:

1. **You** — who, feeling, interests and rhythm.
2. **Your Day** — destination, refinements, map, storyboard and investment.
3. **Make It Yours** — date, pickup, guests, essential details and checkout.

The homepage presents three primary purchase paths: Studio, Signature Experiences and Travel Designer. Moments and Corporate are secondary paths.

## Consent and checkout

- Consent stays a low desktop utility bar and compact mobile bottom sheet. All legal choices remain equally accessible with 44px minimum targets.
- A checkout request shows immediate loading feedback and blocks repeat submission.
- Customer-facing failures use calm, human language and retain a retry action. Raw provider errors are never shown.
- Date, guests, price, inclusions and trust remain visible and legible at the payment seam.
- No ornamental delay may block payment interaction.
