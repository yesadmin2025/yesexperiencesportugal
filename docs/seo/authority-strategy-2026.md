# YES Experiences — 12-month authority strategy (US market)

Published for the owner. Working document, reviewed quarterly.

Honest framing: nobody can guarantee a first-page position. What we can do is
make YES the most complete, most specific and most trusted answer for a small
set of searches American travellers actually make before they buy. That is what
"authority" means to Google: one clear page per intent, updated regularly, cited
by real sites.

## 4. Regular content on each experience page

Each experience page gets a quarterly pass. Twenty minutes per page, four
things only:

1. **Answer the buying questions in the text**: price per person, duration door
   to door, hotel pickup, group size, what happens if it rains.
2. **Refresh the FAQ** with two real questions from the inbox that quarter.
3. **Check the price and season wording** against the live rates.
4. **Add or replace one real photo** from a recent departure.

Priority order for the quarterly pass: Arrábida wine, Sintra & Cascais, Fátima
Nazaré Óbidos, Comporta & Tróia, then the rest.

## 5. Destination articles — priority order

1. 7-day Portugal itinerary (1,300/mo) — sells multi-day and the Travel Designer
2. 10-day Portugal itinerary (480/mo) — same, longer stay
3. Things to do in Lisbon, chosen by locals (12,100/mo) — top of funnel, links down
4. Best time of year for a day trip — evergreen, seasonal traffic
5. Cruise day ashore from Lisbon — high intent, almost no competition from operators
6. Day trips with kids / limited mobility — questions nobody else answers

Each article: one clear target search, one experience it sells, links up to its
hub page and down to the booking page. Never a second page for a search we own.

## 6. Authority links — real targets only

The goal is a handful of genuine mentions per quarter, not a list of hundreds.
Four categories, all verifiable:

- **Portuguese tourism bodies and licences**: Turismo de Portugal operator
  registry, Turismo de Lisboa, Região de Turismo do Alentejo listings.
- **Regional guides that already rank**: Setúbal, Sesimbra, Comporta and
  Arrábida "things to do" pages, plus municipal tourism sites.
- **Wine and food writing**: Moscatel de Setúbal producers we actually work
  with, wine route associations, Portuguese food and wine writers.
- **Travel press and expat media**: Portugal-focused blogs and expat sites with
  a real audience, and travel journalists covering Lisbon day trips.

How to approach, three emails:

1. **First contact** — one paragraph: who we are, licence number, the one guide
   of ours that genuinely helps their readers, no ask beyond a look.
2. **Follow-up (10 days)** — two lines, add one new fact (a new guide, a photo
   set they can use).
3. **Press** — the press angle: a licensed local operator in Sesimbra, the
   Studio that builds a private day in real time, 700+ five-star reviews,
   named contact and response time.

Rules: never buy links, never exchange links in bulk, never pitch a page that
does not deserve the reader's time. One good mention from a regional tourism
site outranks fifty directory entries.

## 7. How we measure

In Admin → SEO monitor, once a week, three numbers:

- **Clicks and average position (28 days vs. previous 28)** — direction matters
  more than the absolute number.
- **Source → payment** — how many Google visits reach the payment step and pay.
- **Bookings per experience** — which pages earn, not just which pages get read.

And once a month: are the retired URLs still redirecting, and did any new page
start competing with a page we already own?

Expect three to six months before consolidation and new content show clearly in
positions. Anything that promises faster is not SEO.
