# Homepage service clarity and conversion plan

## Goal

Make every core YES service immediately understandable and easy to enter, while keeping the homepage cinematic and restrained. Proposals and Corporate will be treated as valuable businesses in their own right, not hidden beneath a generic group label.

## Conversion structure

### 1. Restore five clear ways into YES

Replace the current three-card block and its small secondary links with five complete, equally discoverable paths:

1. **Signature Experiences** — proven private days that can be booked directly.
2. **Experience Studio** — design one private day live, with route and investment visible.
3. **Portugal Travel Designer** — multi-day journeys shaped end to end by a local designer.
4. **Proposals & Celebrations** — discreet, personally planned private moments.
5. **Corporate & Private Groups** — off-sites, incentives, client hosting and group days.

Use the heading **“Five ways into Portugal.”** Each path receives its own title, concise value statement and specific CTA. On mobile, all five remain full, tappable editorial cards—no quiet text links beneath the main choices.

### 2. Use intent-led CTA language

Each service gets a distinct action that tells visitors what happens next:

- Signature: **Explore private days**
- Studio: **Design your day**
- Travel Designer: **Plan your Portugal journey**
- Proposals: **Plan your proposal**
- Corporate: **Plan a private group day**

Destinations, analytics, booking logic and existing product facts remain unchanged.

### 3. Give Proposals a dedicated homepage moment

Create a standalone **Proposals & Celebrations** section with its real imagery, discreet-planning promise, practical support and a direct proposal CTA. Keep romance and milestones together, but make proposals explicit in the heading and action rather than implied by “something special.”

### 4. Give Corporate a separate business section

Move Corporate out of “Bigger plans” and into its own **Corporate & Private Groups** section. State the real use cases clearly: company off-sites, incentives, client hospitality and private groups, with transport, timing, venues and invoicing handled together. Link directly to the existing Corporate page.

### 5. Preserve the main commercial journey

Keep the page sequence conversion-led:

```text
Cinematic hero
→ Trust and reviews
→ Five ways into Portugal
→ Experience Studio
→ Signature Experiences
→ Portugal Travel Designer
→ Proposals & Celebrations
→ Corporate & Private Groups
→ Map, journal, FAQ, final CTA
```

This keeps Studio as the differentiating product and Signature as the fastest booking route, while ensuring Proposals and Corporate are visible before supporting editorial content.

## Mobile presentation

- Show all five services as complete cards with 44px+ actions.
- Use a compact vertical rhythm so clarity does not create a long, repetitive wall.
- Give each card one icon, one promise and one CTA; no nested controls or vague labels.
- Keep Proposals and Corporate visible without swiping or expanding.
- Use the existing ivory, sand, teal, gold, Fraunces and Inter system.

## Copy principles

- Name the service directly before using emotional language.
- Avoid “choose,” “something special,” “bigger plans” and “group” without context.
- Distinguish one-day, multi-day, private occasions and business travel immediately.
- Use real operational strengths only; do not invent venues, inclusions, outcomes or prices.
- Keep the hero’s approved headline and existing product/booking truth unchanged.

## Technical scope

- Rework the homepage service-path component from three primary cards plus hidden links into five first-class paths.
- Split the combined occasions area into two separate homepage sections and update the approved homepage structure contract.
- Reuse the existing editorial card and CTA primitives, existing real images and existing routes.
- Update homepage copy/structure tests and CTA expectations without changing checkout, pricing, inventory or analytics behavior.
- Verify at 393px first, then tablet and desktop; check hierarchy, tap targets, text fit, section order and all five destinations.
